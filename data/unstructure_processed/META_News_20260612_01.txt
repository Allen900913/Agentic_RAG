[NarrativeText] . On June 6, Morgan Stanley analyst Brian Nowak reiterated that Wall Street is overlooking Meta Platforms, Inc. (NASDAQ:META), expanding competitive moat amid massive artificial intelligence spending.

[NarrativeText] According to Nowak, investor sentiment around Meta Platforms has lagged that of other mega-cap technology peers due to limited visibility into the company’s projected $380 billion capital expenditure for 2027 and 2028. The analyst expects the investments to result in products that could add $1 to $3 to the company’s 2028 earnings per share.

[NarrativeText] Nowak expects Meta Platforms to benefit from the multibillion-dollar search opportunity integrated into Meta AI. Should the search tool retain 1 billion users and monetize just 10% of daily queries, it is expected to generate over $10 billion in annual revenue. The company has already begun rolling out tiered subscription packages to pursue high-margin revenue streams.

[NarrativeText] Consequently, Nowak has reiterated that Meta is a top pick among mega caps and reiterated a $775 price target.

[NarrativeText] Meta Platforms, Inc. (NASDAQ:META) is a multinational technology conglomerate that builds digital and physical tools to help people connect. The company operates the world’s largest social media ecosystem, advances artificial intelligence, and develops mixed-reality hardware and wearable devices.
