[NarrativeText] Strategic focus: converting secured power into “time to compute”

[NarrativeText] Co-founder and co-CEO Daniel Roberts framed the company’s strategy around a core constraint in AI infrastructure: the ability to translate secured power into customer-ready compute on tight timelines. Roberts said that while digital demand can scale quickly, physical infrastructure—power, permitting, land, and data centers—takes years to build, creating scarcity that is “now defining where AI infrastructure gets built and who can build it.”
