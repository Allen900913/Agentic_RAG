[Title] The Bear View And The Current Investment Debate

[NarrativeText] The current investment debate on AAPLis centered around:

[NarrativeText] The core disagreement of whether the high-margin Services growth engine is durable enough to overcome global regulators actively trying to dismantle its primary monetization method, the App Store.

[NarrativeText] The prevailing sentiment is neutral. The powerful momentum from the iPhone 17 cycle (+23% growth) and high-margin services is being fully offset by the structural threat from regulators and near-term supply chain headwinds. Sentiment is neutralized by this stalemate.

[Title] Bull View

[Title] Bear View

[NarrativeText] Bulls are betting on continued monetization of the 2.5B+ device installed base, driving services growth >14% and company gross margins >48%, justifying a premium valuation for quality.

[NarrativeText] Bears are focused on the DOJ lawsuit and the EU’s DMA forcing changes to the App Store’s commission model, risking the primary profit driver and causing a significant P/E de-rating.
