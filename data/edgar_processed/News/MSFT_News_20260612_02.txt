. On June 8, Rezolve AI PLC (NASDAQ:RZLV) announced the appointment of Michele Fisher as Chief Marketing Officer. Fisher joins the company from Microsoft, where she served as Global Strategy Director, bringing extensive experience in technology strategy, marketing, and enterprise growth. The appointment reflects Rezolve AI's efforts to strengthen its leadership team as it expands the commercial reach of its artificial intelligence platform and pursues broader adoption across enterprise and retail markets. Earlier, on May 18, Enhanced announced a strategic partnership with Rezolve AI PLC (NASDAQ:RZLV) to help develop the future of its direct-to-consumer telehealth platform, Live Enhanced. The collaboration encompasses AI technologies, digital consumer engagement initiatives, and event sponsorship opportunities. Under the agreement, Rezolve will support the development of AI-powered medical concierge capabilities, intelligent customer engagement tools, and the mobile-first digital infrastructure underpinning Enhanced's consumer platform, further expanding the practical applications of Rezolve's technology portfolio. Rezolve AI PLC (NASDAQ:RZLV) is an enterprise technology company focused on developing generative AI solutions for retail, e-commerce, and enterprise operations. Founded in 2016 and headquartered in London, United Kingdom, the company provides agentic commerce platforms, AI-powered virtual assistants, and automation tools that help businesses improve customer engagement, streamline operations, and facilitate digital transactions.
